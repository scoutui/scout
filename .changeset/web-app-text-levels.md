Column headings and labels are no longer in capitals; they read in sentence case, such as **Uses** and **Chart type**. Words that were shown in the faintest grey, such as a package's scope, a file's folders and **first scan**, now use the same grey as other secondary text, and the faintest grey is kept for dashes and separators. On a repo page, the **Components** and **Adoption** tabs show how many each holds, and on a component's page the **Composition** tab shows how many components render it or are rendered by it. The Governance and Settings pages now sit on the same page width as the other pages, so their titles line up with the rest, while the record form and the people table keep a comfortable width. A repo page's tabs now line up with its title and sit on a rule, like the tabs on a component's page.
