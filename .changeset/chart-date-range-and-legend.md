Charts over time offer a date range (3 months, 6 months, 1 year or All) once their scans span more than 3 months. The range you pick stays in the chart's link, and a saved chart opens at the range picked in the builder when it was saved. Clicking a name in a Trend chart's legend shows only that line. A Trend chart with six or more lines now shows them all on one chart, with a sortable table of each line's latest value in place of the separate small charts.
