After you save or delete a lifecycle record, tag or chart, or a new scan arrives, the **governance** page, the **charts** page, a migration's chart and a repo's **Adoption** tab show **Preparing scan data** above their numbers until the new ones are worked out, then update by themselves. Before, they kept the old numbers with no sign they were out of date until you reloaded. A deleted record's chart now says the page isn't found.
