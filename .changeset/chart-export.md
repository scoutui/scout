A chart's **⋯** menu can now download the chart as a PNG image or copy it to paste into a slide, and download its data as a CSV file or copy it to paste into a spreadsheet. The image is always light, and shows the chart's name, the dates and repos it covers, and each series with its latest value. Exports cover the period picked above the chart. Migration and retirement charts have the menu too, and so does everyone who can open a chart.
