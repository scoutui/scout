A saved chart's **⋯** menu has a new **Export** submenu: **Download PNG** and **Copy image** for a slide or a document, and **Download CSV** and **Copy table** for a spreadsheet. The image is always light, and shows the chart's name, the repos and period it covers, and each series with its latest value. Exports cover the period picked above the chart. Migration and retirement charts list the same items in their **⋯** menu, and everyone who can open a chart can export it.
