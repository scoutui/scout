The chart builder's **Add a series** box now offers components and packages your repos no longer use, after the ones in use, most recently used first. You can chart a component your teams have moved off and watch its uses fall to zero. With one repo picked, the box offers what that repo uses or has used. A package's count now includes the components it no longer uses, so it matches the list you see when you pick the package.
