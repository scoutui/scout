Charts with many series are easier to read. Hovering a chart, the tooltip stays with the pointer and lists the ten largest values at that scan, then how many series it leaves out. A chart's exported image keeps its plot readable however many series it has: a trend names its ten largest lines at their ends with their latest values, a stacked chart's legend lists its eight largest series, and both say how many more there are. When every series comes from one package, the image names that package once under the title.
