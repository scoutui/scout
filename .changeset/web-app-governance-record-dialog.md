On the governance page, Add record and a record's Edit now open a dialog instead of a form on the page. Once you pick a package or component it shows as a card, with Change to pick another. The Replaced and Retired toggle is now a choice of what teams should do instead, "Move to a replacement" or "Retire it", and the chosen one shows its Replacement or Reason field. Create record keeps the dialog open for the next record and counts what you've added; Show lists those records. Closing the dialog with unsaved changes asks whether to discard them first. With no records yet, the form no longer opens on its own: press Add record.
