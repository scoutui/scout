On a saved chart's page, a Trend chart with two or more lines lists them in a table under the chart, with each line's latest uses or share and its change over the last 30 days. With more than 10 lines, the table lists the first 10, with Show all and a search by component or package name; while you search, the chart and its export show only the matching lines. When two components on the chart have the same name, the table shows where each one comes from.
