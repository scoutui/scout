A chart page now has an **Export** button for its image and table, and the **⋯** menu holds only sharing and **Duplicate**, so it's hidden for people who can't use either. In bar charts, a package under a bar's name is no longer cut off at its start, and one too long for the label column breaks after its scope onto another line. The components table on a repo page keeps its **Version**, **Files** and **Uses** headings whole at narrow widths, and on a phone a long component or package name ends in an ellipsis instead of running off the row. A component's **Usage** tab calls its packages or folders **Used in** throughout, the folder or package filter chip says **Used in** too, and on a phone the props under each file wrap instead of being cut off. The Governance page's description and the roles on **Settings → People** are shorter, and the roles now say that Viewers can see governance.
