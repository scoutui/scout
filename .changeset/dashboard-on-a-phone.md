The dashboard fits a phone better. The tabs at the top now sit on their own row under the logo, so all four fit on a phone; on a narrower screen the row scrolls, and its edge fades only while there are more tabs that way. A component's **Used in** list, a repo's **Scan history** and a package's repos no longer scroll sideways: on a phone each row stacks its details under the name, as the other lists do. Column headers you tap to sort and the scan switcher are easier to tap, and tabs, toggles and filter chips now respond when pressed. The sign-in pages sit in the middle of a phone's screen without scrolling, and a count of one now reads "1 use".
