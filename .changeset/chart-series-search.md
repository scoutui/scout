In the chart builder, one **Add a series** box replaces the Tags, Packages and Components tabs. It searches the way Governance's boxes do: the best match comes first, tags and packages before components, and picking a package narrows the search to it, with a row that adds the whole package. The list stays open while you add series. In both places, a long package name shortens its scope rather than losing its end.
