On a phone, the header now shows one button named after the page you're on, such as **packages**, which opens a menu of every page, so no page is cut off at the edge of the screen. Screen readers name the logo Scout, the keyboard focus ring on a tab is no longer cut off, and menus, popovers and dialogs only fade in when your device asks for reduced motion.
