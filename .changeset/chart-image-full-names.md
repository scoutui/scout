A Trend chart's image (**Download PNG** and **Copy image**) now names every line in full. When a package is too long to fit beside its component's name, every package goes on the line beneath its name, so the names read as one list. Two named lines with the same name show the folder that tells them apart. A name is joined to its line only when it had to move clear of another name, and then in that line's colour. The time axis labels the chart's first and last day, on a Stacked chart's image too, and the dot at a line's end no longer cuts a gap in the lines around it.
