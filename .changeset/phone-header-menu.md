On a phone, the header shows a Menu button, the Scout logo and your account. Menu opens a list of the pages you can see, with the one you're on marked. The theme moves from its own button into the account menu, on every screen size. The account menu shows your full name and email, with your role under them. A component's page now marks packages as the current page.
