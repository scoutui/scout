The repo page's component details show a chart of the last 3 months, or of every scan when the repo has fewer than two in that time. A replaced component shows its uses beside its replacement's with the same migrated and left numbers as the **Adoption** tab, and a retired one its uses left, each with **Open chart** to open the full chart for the repo. When records that share a replacement share one chart, it says how many components it counts. Any other component shows its uses in each scan, or a single point when it's new in this scan or the repo has only one scan, with a link to fill in the repo's past scans.
